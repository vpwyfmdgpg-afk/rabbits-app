Privacy Policy

RabbitsKey (Rabbits Keyboard) is made by MS ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. The keyboard does not ask for Full Access, so iOS does not let it use the internet. It cannot send what you type – or anything else – to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Your bunny settings (where it sits, what it does when you stop typing).
  • The emoji you used most recently, to show them first.
  • Words you choose to keep in the suggestion bar, in the iPhone's own spelling dictionary.

None of this leaves your device, and none of it is the text you type.

Suggestions and autocorrect

Suggestions come from the spelling dictionary built into your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is looked up online.
