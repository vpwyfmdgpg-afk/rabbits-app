Privacy Policy

Rabbits is made by MS ("we", "us").

In short

  • No account, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share a sighting on the public map, or switch on the “seen by” count.
  • Shared sightings are deleted after the end of the day.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, your Rabbit spotter checklist, your nickname and your private log stay on your phone. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

When you share a sighting

If you choose to share a sighting on the public map, you send us:

  • the number of rabbits and the time
  • the spot you picked on the map
  • your nickname
  • if you add them: a photo, your Instagram or TikTok name, and a link to your post

We check every sighting before anyone sees it. If we approve it, it shows on the public map until midnight (UK time). Your photo is shown only if you agree.

If you ask for your sighting to be a quest in the Play game and we approve it, it also shows on the map (or only in the game, if you choose), with your photo and nickname, until midnight. Your Instagram or TikTok name is shown only to players aged 16 and over.

After the end of the day, everything you shared is deleted.

Sightings are stored in Apple iCloud, scrambled.

The “seen by” count

If you are 13 or over and switch it on in Me, opening a sighting on the map or in the game adds one to its “Seen by …” number. Only the number is sent – not your name or who you are. It is off unless you switch it on, and the numbers are deleted after the end of the day.

Your location

When you tap "Report a sighting", the app checks once that you are on campus. Your location stays on your phone. It is not saved or sent.

Camera

The AR view uses the camera only to show the campus on your screen. Nothing is recorded.

Wallpapers

The app saves a wallpaper to your Photos only when you ask it to.

Photos you share

When you add a photo to a sighting, the app reads only the photo you pick.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name or payment details.

Videos

Some rabbit videos are on TikTok or YouTube. They are shown only to people aged 16 and over. When you play one, you connect to that service, and its own privacy policy applies.

Your choice

Sharing a sighting is your choice, and we use it only to show it on the map and in the game.

Children

Sharing sightings is for people aged 13 and over.
