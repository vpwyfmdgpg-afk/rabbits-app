Rabbits – help and contact

Rabbits is a guide to the wild rabbits of Leeds campus: a 3D campus map, rabbit areas, sightings shared by other visitors, a small quest game, AR, and wallpapers of the real campus rabbits.

Contact

email m.highers [at] googlemail [dot] com.

Common questions

  • I bought a wallpaper on another device. Open the Shop and tap Restore (or Me › Restore purchases).
  • How do I set a wallpaper? In the Shop, open "How to set a wallpaper".
  • Why can't I share a sighting? Sharing works only on campus, from age 13. You can always save sightings in your private log.
  • How do I report a post? Tap "Report post" on the sighting.
